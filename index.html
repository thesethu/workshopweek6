Where crime is recorded in South Wales

South Wales Police recorded more than 136,000 crimes between September 2025 and September 2026. We mapped every one that could be located, across more than 14,000 streets and public places.

Change in crime

Crime has increased in September 2026, comparing with 12 month ago, but not in the same way in the different regions.

The most common type of crime reported

Violence and sexual offences were the most common type of crime reported by South Wales Police over the last year.
